# Content Style Guide

## General voice

Content should be warm, clear, professional, supportive and honest about availability, schedules and clinical suitability. Use short paragraphs and direct language. Explain before persuading.

## Medical and dental safety

Prefer: may help; can support; may be suitable; depends on clinical evaluation; personalized treatment plan; subject to the dentist's assessment.

Avoid: guaranteed; permanent; lifetime result; 100% success; painless; perfect smile; cure; best for everyone.

## Journey support wording

The platform may explain the journey, coordinate appointments, assist communication, support airport pickup and drop-off, help with hotel and travel planning, and provide practical support during the confirmed patient journey.

Do not imply that the platform diagnoses conditions, determines treatment, replaces a dentist, guarantees travel availability or guarantees treatment outcomes.

## English

Use warm, international, professional plain English. Use “patient journey” consistently for the full experience from first contact through return home.

## Vietnamese

Use a sincere, clear and trustworthy tone. Avoid exaggerated or sensational language. Use “làm răng” in approachable marketing content and “điều trị nha khoa” in more clinical contexts.

## Lao and Thai

Preserve the English meaning and safety level while writing naturally in the target language.

## Formatting

Use sentence case for normal headings, one space after punctuation, no double periods, and avoid excessive exclamation marks.
