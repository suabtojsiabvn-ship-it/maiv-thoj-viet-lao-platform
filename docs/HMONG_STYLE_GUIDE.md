# International Hmong Style Guide

## Audience

Write for the global Hmong community with clear International Hmong. Avoid highly local expressions when a more widely understood phrase is available.

## Core dental terminology

Use **kho hniav** for dental treatment and dental-care context. Avoid inconsistent capitalization such as `kho Hniav`. Do not use `kho mob` for cosmetic or restorative dentistry.

## Names

First formal mention: **A Sử (Txhim)**. Later references: **A Sử**.

Never use A Su, A Sử (Xwm), or A Sử (xwm tsawb).

Use **Dr. Toàn Nguyễn** with Vietnamese diacritics.

## Roles

Maiv Thoj: Community Advisor / International Advisor.  
A Sử: International Patient Journey Manager.

Technical role titles may remain in English when a natural Hmong equivalent would be unclear, but the surrounding explanation should be natural Hmong.

## Tone

Warm, family-oriented, respectful, trustworthy, reassuring and natural. Avoid literal machine-like translation.

## Medical safety

Prefer wording equivalent to “may be suitable,” “depends on the dentist's examination,” and “based on your dental condition.” Avoid guaranteed success, permanent for everyone, painless, or perfect result.

## Free transport policy

Free pickup and drop-off apply throughout the confirmed patient journey. Do not imply that every optional sightseeing trip is free.

## Final review

Check natural International Hmong, correct dental terminology, consistent names and roles, unchanged service scope, no medical guarantee and appropriate capitalization.
