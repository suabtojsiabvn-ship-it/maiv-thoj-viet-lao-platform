# Business Bible

## Brand
Maiv Thoj Viet Lao Platform

## Slogan
A Smile Worth Traveling For

## Primary Audience
Hmong international patients, overseas Vietnamese/Hmong communities, and international patients seeking dental care in Vietnam.

## Core People
- Maiv Thoj: Community Advisor / International Ambassador.
- A Sử (Txhim): International Patient Journey Manager.
- Dr. Toàn Nguyễn: Clinical Director / Dental Expert.
- An Nhien Dental: Main clinical partner in Hà Nội.

## Customer Journey
Social Media / Referral → WhatsApp / Messenger → Initial Consultation → Treatment Planning → Airport Arrival → Accommodation / Support → Clinic Visit → Treatment → Travel Experience → Aftercare → Review / Referral.

## Important Rule
Never communicate as “cheap dental tourism.” Communicate trust, support, and carefully coordinated international patient journey.
