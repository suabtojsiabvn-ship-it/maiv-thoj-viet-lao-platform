# Brand Guide

## 1. Main brand

Use **Maiv Thoj Viet Lao**. The full platform name is **Maiv Thoj Viet Lao Platform**.

Official positioning: **International Medical & Beauty Concierge Platform**  
Primary tagline: **A Smile Worth Traveling For**

## 2. Brand hierarchy

- Platform: Maiv Thoj Viet Lao
- Clinical partner: An Nhien Dental
- Community Advisor / International Advisor: Maiv Thoj
- International Patient Journey Manager: A Sử (Txhim)
- Clinical professional: Dr. Toàn Nguyễn

The platform supports and coordinates the patient journey. Clinical diagnosis and treatment are provided by qualified clinical partners.

## 3. Names and roles

### Maiv Thoj
Use **Maiv Thoj**, **Community Advisor**, **International Advisor**, and **VIP Companion** only for optional support requested in advance and subject to schedule.

Do not use **Community Ambassador** or **Brand Ambassador**.

### A Sử
First or formal mention: **A Sử (Txhim)**. Short references after the first mention: **A Sử**.

Official role: **International Patient Journey Manager**.

Do not describe A Sử as a driver, clinic staff member or doctor.

### Dr. Toàn Nguyễn
Use **Dr. Toàn Nguyễn** in English, Hmong, Lao and Thai. Use **BS. Toàn Nguyễn** in Vietnamese. Keep Vietnamese diacritics.

## 4. Audience priority

Primary audience: **Global Hmong community**.

English remains the source language and current default website locale. International Hmong is the priority translation and audience language.

## 5. Brand voice

The brand should feel warm, trustworthy, calm, personal, professional and culturally respectful. Avoid aggressive, sensational, misleading or medically absolute communication.
