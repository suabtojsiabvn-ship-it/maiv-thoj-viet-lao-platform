---

# Brand Promise

We don't just help you achieve a beautiful smile.

We help you bring home beautiful memories of Vietnam.

---

# Vision

Become the most trusted international healthcare journey platform for overseas patients visiting Vietnam.

---

# Mission

Support patients before, during and after treatment through trusted people, trusted partners and meaningful experiences.

---

# What We Do

We coordinate healthcare journeys.

We connect patients with trusted clinical partners.

We support language, transportation and planning.

We create memorable experiences when treatment schedules allow.

---

# What We Don't Do

We are not a dental clinic.

We are not a travel agency.

We do not replace doctors.

We do not provide medical diagnosis.

# Brand Story

People travel to Vietnam for many reasons.

Some come because they need better dental care.

Some come because they want a new smile.

Many feel nervous before making such an important decision.

Maiv Thoj Viet Lao exists to make that journey easier.

We believe healthcare should begin with trust.

Patients first meet people they trust.

Then they meet trusted healthcare partners.

Along the way they discover Vietnam, its culture and its people.

When they return home, they carry more than a healthy smile.

They carry meaningful memories.
