# Project Decisions

These decisions remain locked until the project owner explicitly changes them.

## DEC-001 — Main brand
**Maiv Thoj Viet Lao**; full name **Maiv Thoj Viet Lao Platform**.

## DEC-002 — Positioning
**International Medical & Beauty Concierge Platform**, initially focused on dental treatment and patient journey support.

## DEC-003 — Primary audience
The global Hmong community, followed by broader international audiences in English, Vietnamese, Lao and Thai.

## DEC-004 — Language strategy
English is the source language and current default website locale. International Hmong is the priority audience translation. Supported order: `en`, `hmn`, `vi`, `lo`, `th`.

## DEC-005 — Maiv Thoj
Approved roles: Community Advisor, International Advisor, and optional VIP Companion by advance request and schedule.

## DEC-006 — A Sử
Formal name: **A Sử (Txhim)**. Short name: **A Sử**. Role: **International Patient Journey Manager**. Do not present him as a driver, doctor or clinic staff member.

## DEC-007 — Clinical partner
Key clinical partner: **An Nhien Dental**. Clinical diagnosis and treatment remain the responsibility of qualified clinical professionals.

## DEC-008 — Doctor name
International: **Dr. Toàn Nguyễn**. Vietnamese: **BS. Toàn Nguyễn**.

## DEC-009 — Hmong dental terminology
Use **kho hniav**. Do not use `kho mob` for cosmetic and restorative dentistry.

## DEC-010 — Pickup and drop-off
Free pickup and drop-off apply throughout the confirmed patient journey, not automatically to every optional tourism activity.

## DEC-011 — Optional travel
Depends on clinical suitability, confirmed treatment schedule, available time and operational availability.

## DEC-012 — Medical claims
Do not publish guarantees such as 100% success, permanent for everyone, lifetime result, painless, or perfect outcome.

## DEC-013 — Tagline
**A Smile Worth Traveling For**.
