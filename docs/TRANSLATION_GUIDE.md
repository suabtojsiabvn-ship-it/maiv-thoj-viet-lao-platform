# Translation Guide

## Language strategy

- Source language: English (`en`)
- Default website locale: English (`en`)
- Priority audience translation: International Hmong (`hmn`)
- Other locales: Vietnamese (`vi`), Lao (`lo`), Thai (`th`)

Translations must be natural rather than literal.

## Policy consistency

Free pickup and drop-off apply throughout the confirmed patient journey. Do not translate this as unlimited free transport for all optional tourism activities.

VIP Companion support is available by advance request and subject to Maiv Thoj's schedule.

Optional travel depends on clinical suitability, the confirmed treatment schedule and available time.

Treatment recommendations require an appropriate clinical evaluation.

## Review checklist

Confirm locked names, approved roles, unchanged service scope, no medical guarantee, matching CTA meaning, natural language, and consistent punctuation.
